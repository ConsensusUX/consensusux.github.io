# Theory

The theoretical foundations of ConsensusUX draw from public sphere theory, anarchist cybernetics, consensus decision-making traditions, decolonial design, and design justice. This section documents the academic underpinnings and design philosophy that inform every feature in the application.

ConsensusUX is not arbitrary. Every design decision — from the absence of admin privileges to the structure of the vouching system, from identity segmentation to the soft lock dispute mechanism — traces back to specific theoretical commitments. The application is an argument made in software: that participatory, horizontal, and equitable digital infrastructure is both possible and necessary.
